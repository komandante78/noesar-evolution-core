# European Union Compliance Pack

Initial mapping covers GDPR, AI Act, Cyber Resilience Act, NIS2, DORA where
applicable, MDR/IVDR for medical modules and relevant sector rules.

GDPR controls include purpose/legal basis, minimisation, rights, DPIA support,
records, role configuration, retention/deletion, transfers, incident evidence
and privacy by design.

AI Act controls include actor/risk classification, intended purpose, prohibited
use screening, transparency, human oversight, technical documentation, logs,
data governance, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity and post-market controls
where applicable. Dates/classifications live in signed packs, not hard-coded.
