# Other Jurisdiction Packs

The architecture supports UK, Canada, Brazil, Japan, Singapore, Australia,
India, South Korea, Switzerland, China and additional jurisdictions. Each pack
uses official sources and qualified local counsel.

A missing pack means `LEGAL_AUTOMATION_AVAILABLE=false` and
`CUSTOMER_REVIEW_REQUIRED=true`. It does not automatically prohibit lawful use,
but NOESAR cannot claim configuration coverage.
