# Privacy and Data Governance

Data classes: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted, Personal, Sensitive
Personal, Health, Financial, Export-Controlled, Safety-Critical and Secret.
Controls: inventory, purpose, legal basis, consent, minimisation, encryption,
access, retention, legal hold, correction, export, deletion propagation,
residency, pseudonymisation, connector restrictions, training restrictions and
audit.

Self-hosting is not automatic anonymisation or compliance. Fine-tuning on
customer data requires policy, provenance and deletion-impact analysis.
