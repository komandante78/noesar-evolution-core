# Global Compliance Architecture

NOESAR provides configurable controls and evidence, not a universal legal
conclusion. A signed compliance pack contains jurisdiction, dates, role
definitions, questionnaire, mappings, notices, evidence templates, retention,
prohibited configurations, review date and sources.

The deployment records possible roles such as controller, processor, provider,
deployer, importer, distributor, model provider, employer, healthcare entity
and financial institution. The customer and counsel confirm classification.
