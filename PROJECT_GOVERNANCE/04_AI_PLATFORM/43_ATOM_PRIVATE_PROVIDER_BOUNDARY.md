# ATOM Evolution Private Provider Boundary

ATOM is a proprietary reasoning/orchestration provider. The public
`ReasoningProvider` contract exposes plan generation, constraints, causal
hypotheses, confidence, evidence, simulation, safety classification,
cancellation, audit and deterministic fixtures.

The public core includes a functional ReferenceReasoningProvider. Private ATOM
may implement L0–L8 maturity, proprietary causal search, heuristics,
evolutionary evaluation and protected optimizations. Public builds and funded
demonstrations cannot require or expose ATOM.
