# Model Governance

Every model is quarantined and described by identity, publisher, source,
license, intended/excluded use, architecture, format, precision, quantization,
hash/signature, model card, data provenance where available, commercial
constraints, adapters, RAM/VRAM, context, benchmarks, privacy/safety risks,
advisories and rollback compatibility.

Lifecycle: discover, inspect license, verify publisher/hash/signature, scan,
plan compatibility, benchmark, approve, activate, monitor, update/revoke/rollback.
No silent model download or activation. Remote models are connectors and change
the privacy indicator.
