# Industry Module Trust Levels

- NOESAR Official: built/signed/supported by NOESAR.
- NOESAR Certified Partner: built by an authorised partner under a certification agreement.
- Customer Private: proprietary internal module, not automatically certified.
- Community: third-party module, strongly sandboxed and disabled by default in high-risk profiles.

Medical, surgical, aerospace, live-financial and critical-infrastructure
profiles may allow only Official or explicitly reviewed Customer Private
modules.
