# Customer Private Modules

Customers may build proprietary modules through the public SDK. Requirements:
private publisher identity, organization scope, license, signed package,
permissions, sandbox profile, compatibility, security scan, tests, approval,
update/rollback and support owner.

Customer modules cannot use Official/Certified marks without agreement, bypass
core invariants, embed NOESAR private keys, claim certification from mere
installation, access unrelated tenants or silently activate external transfer.
NOESAR may offer paid design, validation and certification services.
