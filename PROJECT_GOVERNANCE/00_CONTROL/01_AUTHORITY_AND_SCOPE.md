# Authority and Scope

Owner decisions have highest product authority but do not override law,
third-party licenses or non-bypassable safety invariants.

In scope: architecture, repositories, licensing, security, privacy, hardware,
models, data, capabilities, industry modules, Enterprise readiness, funding and
final packaging.

Out of scope: production code, final legal contracts, certification, clinical
validation, flight qualification, live trading, model downloads and deployment.

Conflicts must record sources, affected requirements, legal/security impact,
recommendation and Owner decision.
