# Product Boundary and Claims

Allowed core claims: self-hosted, local-first, customer-controlled, extensible,
hardware-aware and operable without mandatory NOESAR cloud services.

Conditional claims require exact release evidence: offline, air-gap, runtime
compatibility, multi-GPU, HA, accessibility and reproducible builds.

Prohibited unqualified claims include GDPR/AI Act/HIPAA/FDA certification,
medical or flight certification, guaranteed returns, hacker-proof and absolute
no-egress claims. Regulatory status belongs to a specific module, intended use,
release and deployment.
