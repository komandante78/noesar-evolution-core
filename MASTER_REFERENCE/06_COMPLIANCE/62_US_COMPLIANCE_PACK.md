# United States Compliance Pack

US configuration is sector/state-specific. Potential mappings include HIPAA,
FDA requirements, 21 CFR Part 11, state privacy and automated-decision rules,
financial model risk, consumer protection, anti-discrimination, export and
sanctions controls.

NOESAR supports role selection, data separation, audit/retention/access
evidence and reviewed claims. Clinical, device and live-financial use requires a
separate regulated module. State and sector packs carry effective dates.
