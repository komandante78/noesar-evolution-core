# Editions, Licensing and Continuity

Open source: public core under AGPL. Commercial Base: up to five named users,
no key and offline capable. Pro: up to one hundred users. Enterprise:
contract-defined scale, offline entitlement and optional private services.

A signed three-calendar-month launch entitlement may unlock premium features.
Expiration enters Restricted Continuity, preserving personal authentication,
documents, edit/export/backup, projects, local chat and five continuity users.
No entitlement can delete, encrypt, trap or remotely disable customer data.
