# Identity and Collaboration

Support local accounts, passkeys, MFA, OIDC, Enterprise SAML/SCIM, service
accounts, workload identities and Owner recovery. Combine RBAC and ABAC.
Roles: User, Professional, Developer, Workspace Admin, Security Admin, Owner.

Collaboration includes personal, project and organization spaces, approval
queues, review, version history and exportable audit. External sharing is off by
default. The last Owner cannot be removed before another verified Owner exists.
