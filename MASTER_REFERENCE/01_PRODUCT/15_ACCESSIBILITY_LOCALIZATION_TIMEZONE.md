# Accessibility, Localization and Timezone

English is canonical. Language detection order: user, organization, browser,
OS, installation, English fallback. Timestamps are stored in UTC and rendered
with IANA timezone identifiers and DST-safe schedules.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard operation, screen readers, visible focus, scaling,
reduced motion, high contrast, RTL-ready layout, accessible charts and recovery
instructions.
