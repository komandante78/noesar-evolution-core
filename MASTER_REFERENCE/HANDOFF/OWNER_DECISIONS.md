# Owner Decisions Incorporated

- Approved professional WebUI is the visual baseline.
- NOESAR is local-first and self-hosted.
- Chat and CodeN show the verified local privacy statement.
- CodeN requires path consent and offers scoped Owner Bypass.
- Only the Owner installation receives the special unlocked entitlement.
- Non-bypassable safety and anti-malware controls remain.
- Hardware/model selection is automatic, flexible and configurable.
- RAM, VRAM, multi-GPU and future technologies use adapters.
- Technology Radar and signed update notifications are mandatory.
- Industry modules are built after the core and separately validated.
- Customers may build private modules through the SDK.
- The project has open-source and commercial distribution.
- ATOM and sensitive commercial components remain private.
- Public grant work must be useful without private code.
- The project is rewritten before product ZIP creation.
