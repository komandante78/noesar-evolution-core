# Dual-Licensing Strategy

The same public core code is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later or the NOESAR
Commercial License. AGPL users may study, use, modify and redistribute under
AGPL conditions. Commercial customers obtain alternative rights for closed
integration, redistribution, support and Enterprise use.

File-level exceptions: SDKs/schemas/interoperability contracts may use
Apache-2.0; public documentation may use CC BY-SA 4.0; third-party assets keep
their licenses; private extensions are proprietary. These are artifact-specific
licenses, not extra product editions.

Required controls: SPDX headers, copyright inventory, CLA, DCO, notices, patent
disclosure, trademark policy and specialist legal review. This architecture is
not the final legal agreement.
