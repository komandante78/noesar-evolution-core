# Contributions, CLA and DCO

Contributors accept terms, sign off provenance through DCO and grant sufficient
non-exclusive rights through a counsel-reviewed CLA for public and commercial
distribution. Contributors retain copyright unless separately agreed. CI
validates sign-off, license headers and origin; security-sensitive work gets
additional review.

The CLA cannot revoke the public FOSS license of accepted contributions. No
external code contribution is accepted before the contribution documents are
final.
