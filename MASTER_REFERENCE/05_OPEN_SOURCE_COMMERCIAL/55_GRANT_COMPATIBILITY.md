# Grant Compatibility

NLnet/Open Internet Stack work must be FOSS, independently useful, publicly
buildable and free of ATOM or private-service dependencies. The project should
use measurable components rather than one indivisible promise.

Sovereign Tech candidates should be reusable open digital infrastructure with
freely modifiable and redistributable code/documentation.

Mandatory grant controls: public repository, open license, public docs,
milestones, evidence, funding and patent disclosure, no double funding,
security review and sustainability. Commercial products may consume the open
components without changing their open status.
