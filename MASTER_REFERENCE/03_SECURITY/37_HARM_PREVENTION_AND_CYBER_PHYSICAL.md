# Harm Prevention and Cyber-Physical Boundary

Action levels: L0 informational, L1 reversible personal, L2 bounded project,
L3 host/server, L4 destructive/security-sensitive, L5 human or physical impact.
L2 requires plan and authority; L3 strong confirmation and rollback; L4 step-up
and dual confirmation; L5 human control plus external safety interlock.

NOESAR is never the sole safety controller for surgical tools, aircraft,
vehicles, industrial machinery or energy systems. Actuation modules are off by
default and require separate validation, allowlists, rate/range limits and
emergency disconnect.
