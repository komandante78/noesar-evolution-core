## 3. Points requiring attention

- **`r-efi` 5.3.0 and 6.0.0** — `MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later`. The
  expression is disjunctive, so MIT or Apache-2.0 may be selected and LGPL
  obligations avoided. **The chosen option must be recorded explicitly** in the
  final notices rather than left to the reader.
- **`Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception`** (3 crates) — the exception text must be
  reproduced alongside Apache-2.0, not folded into it.
- **`(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0`** (1 crate) — conjunctive: the
  Unicode-3.0 terms apply *in addition* and must be reproduced.
- **`Apache-2.0 OR BSL-1.0`** (1 crate) — Boost licence has its own notice form if
  selected.
- **`MIT AND BSD-3-Clause`** (1 crate) — conjunctive; both notices required.
- **Apache-2.0 §4(d)** — where a crate ships a `NOTICE` file, its contents must be
  propagated into the distributed notices.

## 4. Compatibility with the proposed open core

The proposed core licence is `AGPL-3.0-or-later` (a proposal — see
`docs/LICENSE_STRATEGY.md`). Permissive dependencies (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD,
Unlicense, BSL) are generally compatible with distribution under AGPL-3.0-or-later,
with Apache-2.0 requiring GPLv3-family compatibility, which AGPL-3.0-or-later
provides.

**No dependency in this tree is known to block the proposed licensing.** That is an
engineering observation from declared metadata, **not** a legal conclusion.

## 6. Method and limitations

Generated by reading each crate's `Cargo.toml` `license` field and enumerating
shipped `LICENSE*` / `COPYING*` / `NOTICE*` files. Limitations:

- declared metadata was trusted; per-file copyright headers inside vendored crates
  were **not** audited individually;
- no verbatim licence text is reproduced yet;
- one row in `LICENSE_INVENTORY.tsv` (`capabilities/security/license-policy.json`)
  is a filename-pattern false positive — it is a policy file, not a licence;
- copyright holders were not enumerated per crate.

A complete notices file with verbatim texts and attributions is a **Phase 5**
deliverable.
